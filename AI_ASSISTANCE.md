# AI assistance disclosure

The project owner used ChatGPT/Codex as a development aid for research, alternative analysis,
source-code generation and modification assistance, test design, adversarial review, documentation
editing, and local command execution.
AI suggestions were not treated as evidence by themselves: product claims require source-linked
reasoning or reproducible unit, real-MySQL, packaging, and CI results.

The owner remains responsible for:

- the problem definition, scope, public identity, license, release, and contest submission;
- reviewing the final submitted diff and understanding the core behavior, generated evidence,
  safety boundaries, and direct-dependency roles well enough to explain them;
- reproducing the build and explaining callback semantics, concurrency limits, privacy trade-offs,
  manifest policy, failure boundaries, and prior-art differences;
- accepting or rejecting generated suggestions and correcting unsupported claims;
- community communication, issue decisions, approvals, and presentation.

No AI model, generated inference, training dataset, or external model API is part of RouteContract's
runtime product. This file describes the development process; it does not convert AI review into an
independent user, maintainer endorsement, upstream acceptance, or test result.
